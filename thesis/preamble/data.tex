%% Abstract (recommended length around 80-200 words; this is not a copy of your thesis assignment!)
\newcommand*{\Abstract}{%
	This thesis studies randomized numerical linear algebra in mixed precision,
	with a later application to multivariate climate-model post-processing. The
	main mathematical objective is to analyze how randomized approximation and
	finite-precision perturbations interact when empirical covariance operators
	are compressed and used to construct a regularized dependence-correction
	map. Least-squares sketching is treated as the first model setting in which
	these effects can be understood geometrically and numerically. The later
	climate-facing part uses this viewpoint to formulate a covariance-based
	transport step for multivariate bias correction, with emphasis on
	conditioning, regularization, and the tradeoff between randomized and
	arithmetic error.
}
