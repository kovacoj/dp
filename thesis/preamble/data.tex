%% Abstract (recommended length around 80-200 words; this is not a copy of your thesis assignment!)
\newcommand*{\Abstract}{%
    This thesis studies statistical post-processing of multidimensional climate
    data with emphasis on methods for correcting and analysing climate-model
    output. The work is motivated by the need to reduce systematic errors in
    simulations while preserving realistic dependence structures across
    variables, space, and time. The thesis combines a review of relevant
    statistical methods with background on randomized numerical linear algebra
    and mixed-precision arithmetic, especially where these tools may help with
    large-scale multivariate datasets. The application part focuses on regional
    climate-model data for Central Europe and evaluates both statistical quality
    and numerical aspects of the proposed workflow.
}
