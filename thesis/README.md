## Assignment Summary

The thesis studies statistical post-processing of multidimensional climate data, with emphasis on modern methods for correcting and analysing outputs of climate models.

The planned work starts with a review of randomized numerical linear algebra and mixed-precision arithmetic, especially where these methods may help with large climate datasets and multivariate time-series post-processing.

The application part focuses on building and evaluating a method for multivariate post-processing that respects local temporal behaviour, inter-variable dependence, and spatial structure. Validation is to be carried out on regional climate-model data for Central Europe, with attention to statistical agreement, dependence structure, numerical stability, approximation errors, and finite-precision effects.
